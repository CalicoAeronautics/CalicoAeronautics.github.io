Why this exists

Physics shouldn't be hidden behind impossible textbooks.

It should inspire curiosity, not gatekeep it. That's the whole premise of Calico Physics.

Mission

Make the ideas legible, not just the equations

Most physics resources assume you already understand the notation before they'll explain the idea behind it. Calico Physics starts from the intuition first - the equation comes after, as a shorthand for something you already understand.

Philosophy

Curiosity is the prerequisite, not prior coursework

You shouldn't need a physics degree to be curious about black holes, or a math degree to wonder what imaginary numbers actually are. Every article assumes interest, not background.

Approach

Playful, but never dumbed down

A calico cat wandering across the page doesn't mean the physics is watered down - it means the site doesn't take itself too seriously while taking the ideas very seriously.

Scope

A place to learn, practice, and compete

Articles for understanding, calculators and simulations for intuition, and an Olympiad Hub for anyone who wants to test what they've learned against real competition problems.
