Physics shouldn't be hidden behind impossible textbooks.

Curiosity should be the only requirement. That's the whole premise of Calico Aeronautics.

Mission

Ideas come before equations

Calico Aeronautics starts with intuition. The equation shows up afterward, as shorthand for something you already understand.

Philosophy

Curiosity is the only prerequisite

You can be curious about how a wing generates lift without an engineering degree. Every page here assumes interest and builds the background in as it goes.

Approach

Playful, and genuinely rigorous

A calico cat wanders across the page and keeps things light. The physics underneath it stays accurate.

Scope

Built around flight

Mechanics, aerodynamics, and thermodynamics, with a real emphasis on aeronautics - the equations behind lift and drag, the history of flight from da Vinci's sketches to electric aircraft, and the people who made it possible.
