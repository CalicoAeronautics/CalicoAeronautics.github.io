Recommended Reading

Free, Public Domain

Aerodynamics for Naval Aviators

H.H. Hurt Jr. - the classic US Navy training text on applied aerodynamics, free to read in full.

Stick and Rudder

Wolfgang Langewiesche - still considered the definitive explanation of how and why airplanes actually fly.

Understanding Flight

Anderson & Eberhardt - a clear, physically accurate explanation of lift that cuts through common myths.
