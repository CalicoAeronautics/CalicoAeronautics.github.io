IPhyC

The International Physics Competition - a real, free, online physics contest for secondary school and university students worldwide.

Round 1

Qualification

Five problems (A through E) spanning a wide range of physics topics and difficulty levels. All you need is a pen, paper, and an internet connection.

Round 2

Semi-Final

Qualifiers move on to a deeper round of problems. A small registration fee applies, with financial aid available for students who need it.

Round 3

Final

The top tier of the competition, including research-based and conceptual problems. Top scorers earn National Awards and international recognition.

Recognition

Awards & Certificates

Every participant receives a certificate. Top performers in each country earn a National Award, and the highest scorers overall earn medals and prizes.

What makes it different: IPhyC problems draw on real research topics rather than just standard curriculum material, and the whole competition runs online, so it's open to students anywhere with an internet connection.

Full rules, current dates, and registration are on the official site: iphyc.org.
