Real aerospace and aviation competitions worth knowing about, whether you're in high school or already in a university engineering program.

Aerospace · University

AIAA Design/Build/Fly

Running since 1996, teams design, build, and fly a radio-controlled electric aircraft to meet a new mission profile each year, then compete head-to-head at a fly-off in Wichita, Kansas.

Official site
Aerospace · Middle School to University

NASA Student Launch

A nine-month NASA engineering challenge: design, build, and launch a high-powered rocket with a scientific or engineering payload to a target altitude, going through the same design-review process NASA missions use.
