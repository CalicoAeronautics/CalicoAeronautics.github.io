# Black Holes 101: The Point of No Return

*9 min read &middot; Astronomy &middot; Black Holes, Relativity, Spacetime*

A black hole isn't a hole, and it isn't empty. It's an enormous amount of mass compressed into a small enough space that its escape velocity exceeds the speed of light.

## The event horizon

The event horizon is the boundary past which nothing - not even light - can escape. It isn't a physical surface; it's a point of no return defined purely by geometry.

Cross it, and every possible path forward leads inward. There is, quite literally, no direction you can travel that leads back out.

## Time near a black hole

To a distant observer, a clock falling toward a black hole appears to tick slower and slower, seeming to freeze at the horizon. To the person actually falling in, their own clock ticks normally - they cross the horizon in finite time, unaware anything dramatic just happened.
