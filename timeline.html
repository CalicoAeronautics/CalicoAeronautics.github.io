History, in order

Timeline of Physics

Scroll down. Each idea builds on the one before it.

c. 600-300 BCE

Ancient Greece

Thales, Pythagoras, and Aristotle lay the first systematic groundwork for observing and reasoning about the natural world, mixing early physics with philosophy.

1687

Newton

Isaac Newton publishes the Principia, unifying motion on Earth and in the heavens under the same three laws and a single law of gravitation.

1860s-1870s

Maxwell

James Clerk Maxwell unifies electricity, magnetism, and light into one set of equations, predicting electromagnetic waves before they're ever observed.

1905-1915

Einstein

Albert Einstein publishes Special Relativity, then General Relativity, replacing Newton's gravity with curved spacetime and reshaping our idea of space and time itself.

1900-1930s

Quantum Mechanics

Planck, Bohr, Heisenberg, Schrödinger, and Dirac build a new framework for the atomic scale, where probability, not certainty, governs the rules.

Today

Today

Physicists chase gravitational waves, dark matter, quantum computers, and a still-elusive theory that unifies gravity with the quantum world.
